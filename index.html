04 / A little about me

Hello, I'm Aadil.

I came to singing without formal training for a long time, and later studied classical music. These days, I move between singing, writing and producing, often getting caught up in a lyric or a small detail in an arrangement until it feels right.

Tum Jaisa is one song from that journey. This site is where I’ll keep the releases, the live moments and the things that happen between them. I’m not going to pretend I have the whole story figured out. I’m just going to keep making music and let you hear it as it comes.
